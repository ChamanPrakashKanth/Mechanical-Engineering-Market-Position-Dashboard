Semiconductor supply chain

Photoresists, process gases and lithography feed wafer processing, followed by assembly and test. Process geography, equipment IP and packaging capability are separate questions.

Which semiconductor steps need separate evidence?

Wafer fabrication, equipment production, chemical inputs and packaging each need their own facility and source records. A domestic assembly plant does not prove domestic wafer or tool supply.
