How does a dependency scenario differ from a risk forecast?

The scenario follows the model's directed edges. Exposure is conditional on assumed input use and disruption; it is not a probability, output-loss estimate or statement that a country lacks substitutes. Unknown evidence is never interpreted as resilience.

Does local production mean supply-chain independence?

No. The origin of inputs, equipment, qualification and supplier contracts requires separate evidence. Nameplate capacity, installed capacity and actual output are different measures.

Can these records identify a live shortage?

No. Reports describe their cited periods. Current shortage, price and lead-time claims require fresh product-specific sources.
