MechIntel CAD starter library
Original educational geometry, released under CC0 1.0 Universal.
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

All dimensions are millimetres. STL does not encode units: select mm when importing.
STL is a faceted mesh (96 segments around circles), not editable STEP or a native feature tree.
Open the .scad sources in OpenSCAD to change the dimensions and regenerate geometry.
The four models have no tolerances, threads, chamfers, fits, material specifications, or load ratings.
They are not representations of standardized catalog hardware. Check dimensions and suitability before fabrication.
SVG previews are illustrative and are not dimensioned manufacturing drawings.
