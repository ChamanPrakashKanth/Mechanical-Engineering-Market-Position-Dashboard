Questions about nickel superalloys

Can the stages be used as a country ranking?

No. Read the source scope, record type and quantity together. Facility output, nameplate capacity, model parameters and research demonstrations measure different things.

What does missing evidence mean?

A research gap does not establish absence. A verified-absence statement would require a dated source and explicit scope.

How should source dates be read?

Publication and access dates are separate. Undated publications have unknown freshness. An access date does not independently verify continued operation.
