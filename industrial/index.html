Capability records

Technical milestones and research reviews have separate record types. An evidence review expands coverage and does not establish that production improved on the review date.

Evidence coverage and assessment rules

Stage assignments are editorial assessments of documented domestic production within each capability definition. A dark-green module factory, for example, is assessed for module manufacturing. Component origin and upstream dependencies require their own evidence.
