Feature layout and design notes

Single generic finger blank. Print two copies for a mirrored opposing layout, then determine the actual gripper adapter separately. Two foot slots span Y6–26 mm with X centers 7.25 and 16.75 mm. The raised contact end is Y46–70 mm and has two 10 × 3 mm strap passages. It matches the separately downloadable pad outline, with no positive pad latch. Fixed offsets must be reviewed after parameter edits. No motor, linkage, sensor, fasteners or proprietary mounting pattern is included. Finger length changes leverage at the gripper base; consult actual manufacturer limits before designing an adapter. Print broad 24 × 70 mm back face on the bed, step upward. Original geometry, not a supplier replacement. All mounting apertures are rectangular slots. No physical print, gripping-force, fatigue, friction, retention or environmental tests have been performed. Dimensions are nominal: measure the actual hardware and add process-specific allowance. No powered-robot use, payload rating or standards compliance is established. Use only for unpowered bench fit and layout evaluation.
