What a sponsorship could support

  • Maintenance of the free browser-based tools.
  • Original educational examples and project guides.
  • Clearly labeled sponsor acknowledgement on an agreed relevant page.

Placement, duration, fee and reporting would be agreed in writing before any campaign begins. No minimum reach or result is promised.

What stays independent

  • Tools, lessons and downloads stay free.
  • Sponsors cannot buy better scores or rankings.
  • Personal resumes and profile details are not offered to sponsors.
  • Commercial placements must be clearly labeled.

A sponsorship does not certify a product or imply a hiring outcome.

Audience and measurement

The project is designed for engineering students and early-career professionals, with detailed mechanical engineering resources. That describes the intended audience, not verified visitor demographics.

No verified traffic figures or active sponsor relationships are published on this page. A potential partner should request an agreed measurement plan before making a commitment. Only appropriate aggregate reporting would be considered.
