How do I calculate shim washer dimensions?

The inner diameter equals the reference shaft diameter plus the chosen diametral clearance. Thickness is the axial adjustment supplied by one shim. Use a measured assembly gap to select thickness; this tool does not measure the gap or recommend a fit.

Worked example: 0.5 mm shim

For OD 30 mm, reference diameter 16 mm, diametral clearance 0.2 mm and thickness 0.5 mm, the bore is 16.2 mm. Volume is approximately 0.2504 cm³ and ideal solid mass is approximately 1.965 g at 7.85 g/cm³.

What do the downloads contain?

OpenSCAD: an editable solid ring with bore, outside diameter and length parameters in millimetres. Open the source in OpenSCAD and render/export STL locally if needed.

DXF: two concentric circles forming the nominal front profile, with millimetre unit metadata. Thickness is not represented in this 2D file. Confirm import units in your CAD/CAM software. These downloads contain no threads, chamfers, tolerances or STEP geometry.

Clearance is diametral: radial gap equals half of it. Mass assumes homogeneous solid material and excludes printed infill or process compensation. Material density is your input.

Formulas and assumptions

Annular area = π(OD² − bore²)/4. Volume = area × length. Convert mm³ to cm³ by dividing by 1000. Mass in grams = volume in cm³ × density in g/cm³. For the axial assembly, C = H − 2B − S and worst-case tolerance = T_H + 2T_B + T_S.

These are nominal educational geometry and dimension-chain tools. They do not certify strength, sealing, bearing preload, material selection or manufacturing suitability.
