Feature layout and design notes

Blade-holder geometry study with a continuous smooth slot for a separately designed scraper blade, a reinforced rear web and two base mounting slots with 25 mm travel. Two Ø5.5 holes through the rear web are generic clamp-fastener clearances; no clamp screws or compliant blade are supplied. This is not a belt-cleaning assembly and has no wear, food-contact, belt-contact, torque or safety rating. Verify blade section, retention, belt clearance, debris behavior and manufacturer guidance before any real installation.
