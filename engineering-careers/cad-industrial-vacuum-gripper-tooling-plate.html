Feature layout and design notes

Mechanical tooling plate only: no internal pneumatic passages, sealing surfaces or suction cups. Four horizontal slots have endpoint pairs X15–40 and X80–105 at Y20/60, width8.5 and 25 mm endpoint travel. Four Ø5.5 holes are on a 30 × 30 mm square at X45/75/Y25/55. Central Ø20 opening is a clearance passage. Confirm cup stems, hose routing, payload, robot flange interface and fasteners separately; no vacuum or lifting rating.
