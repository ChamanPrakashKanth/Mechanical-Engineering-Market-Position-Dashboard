The strongest evidence is a device prototype, biomedical signal analysis, or documented risk-control file. A certificate can support evidence, but should not replace it.

How do I know whether it improved my opportunities?

Compare meaningful signals week over week: suitable roles found, tailored applications sent, replies, referrals, screening calls, interviews and portfolio reviews. Do not treat a readiness score as a hiring prediction.

Evidence boundary: Role and skill lists are editorial starting points, not live labor-market measurements. Verify requirements against current vacancies, employer career pages, professional bodies and applicable regulations.
