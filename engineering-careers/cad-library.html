What is included?

Each part includes an STL mesh and an editable .scad source. Circles use 96 segments. STL is a faceted format and does not encode units: select millimetres when importing. These downloads do not include STEP or native SolidWorks files.

These are simplified educational models, not standardized catalog hardware. No tolerances, material properties, threads, chamfers, fits or load ratings are specified. Confirm suitability before fabrication.

How do I change the dimensions?

  1. Download a part’s OpenSCAD source.
  2. Open it in OpenSCAD and edit the named dimensions at the top. Keep dimensions positive; for hollow parts, keep the inside diameter smaller than the outside diameter.
  3. Render the model and export a new mesh. Check dimensions in your CAD or slicing application.
