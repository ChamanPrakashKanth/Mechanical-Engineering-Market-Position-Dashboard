Feature layout and design notes

A separate contact-pad geometry study matching the 24 × 24 mm end of the stepped finger. Two strap slots align when its origin is translated by Y46/Z20 relative to the finger. Relief grooves do not establish extra friction or gripping force. Separate straps are required; routing them over the contact face can interfere with the workpiece. TPU may be evaluated for compliance using the filament supplier settings; material suitability and retention are unverified. No adhesive or fastener is included. Print flat back down and grooves upward. Measure the assembled stack and evaluate creep and contact pressure on an unpowered bench. Original geometry, not a supplier replacement. All mounting apertures are rectangular slots. No physical print, gripping-force, fatigue, friction, retention or environmental tests have been performed. Dimensions are nominal: measure the actual hardware and add process-specific allowance. No powered-robot use, payload rating or standards compliance is established. Use only for unpowered bench fit and layout evaluation.
