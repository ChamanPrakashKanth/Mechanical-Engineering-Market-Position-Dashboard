How much clearance should a 3D-printed hole have?

There is no universal value. Print a small coupon using the material, orientation and slicer settings intended for the final part, then try the actual mating shaft. This generator explores a range you choose; it does not prescribe a fit class.

Worked example: an 8 mm shaft

With an 8.00 mm measured shaft, a first diametral clearance of 0.00 mm, a 0.10 mm increment and six holes, the CAD diameters are 8.00, 8.10, 8.20, 8.30, 8.40 and 8.50 mm. A nominal 8.30 mm hole has 0.30 mm diametral clearance, or 0.15 mm per side relative to that shaft. The printed result must be checked.

Print and measure in four steps

  1. Measure the actual shaft. Enter its diameter and a clearance range that brackets your intended fit.
  2. Download the OpenSCAD source, render it and export STL. Print flat with the hole axes vertical. Keep the same settings you plan to use for the mating feature.
  3. Let the coupon cool, remove burrs consistently, then try each hole without forcing the shaft. Record the fit. Use suitable measuring equipment if you need reliable internal dimensions.
  4. Confirm the chosen clearance on a representative segment of the final part. Orientation, wall thickness, material and feature size can change the result.
Formula: hole diameter = measured shaft diameter + first clearance + (hole number − 1) × increment. Measured clearance = printed hole diameter − measured shaft diameter.

Frequently asked questions

Is 0.30 mm clearance the same as 0.30 mm per side?

No. This tool uses diametral clearance. A 0.30 mm diameter difference corresponds to a radial gap of 0.15 mm per side for ideal concentric circles.

Can this generate bearing-fit or press-fit test pieces?

You can enter a measured mating diameter and include negative nominal clearances. A simple coupon does not establish retention force, bearing preload, material strength or a standardized engineering fit. Confirm performance using the actual geometry and material.

Does the downloaded source contain text or supports?

The source contains a rectangular plate with circular through-holes. It has no embossed labels, compensation, supports or production ratings. Label hole 1 manually and inspect the slicer preview.

Can I use it offline?

Yes. Save this complete HTML page. Calculations, preview and OpenSCAD/CSV downloads work locally without external libraries.
