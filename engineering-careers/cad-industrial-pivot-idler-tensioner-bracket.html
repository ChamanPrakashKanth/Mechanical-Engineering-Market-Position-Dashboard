Feature layout and design notes

Concept bracket with a base, paired side cheeks, coaxial pivot passage and a curved row of locking-hole positions around the pivot. The opposite cross-hole is a smooth roller-axle clearance. Model dimensions do not define a bearing, bolt preload, idler size or conveyor belt tension. Check pivot shear, cheek bending, roller alignment, adjustment range, fastener retention and machine guarding; no operating load or belt-tracking performance is claimed.
