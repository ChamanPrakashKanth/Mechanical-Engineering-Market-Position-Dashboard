Feature layout and design notes

Three pockets store loose generic finger blanks upright on a bench, not an assembled robot end effector. Each 26 × 10 mm pocket leaves 2 mm nominal total width and thickness allowance around a measured 24 × 8 mm plain finger end. The raised finger end remains above the rack. Pocket origins X3,32,61/Y20 mm, depth21 mm. Floor thickness is 4 mm. Two through mounting slots accept separately selected hardware and washers. Fit, tipping stability and retention are untested; do not use this as a robot docking station or tool changer. Print flat underside down, pockets upward. Original geometry, not a supplier replacement. All mounting apertures are rectangular slots. No physical print, gripping-force, fatigue, friction, retention or environmental tests have been performed. Dimensions are nominal: measure the actual hardware and add process-specific allowance. No powered-robot use, payload rating or standards compliance is established. Use only for unpowered bench fit and layout evaluation.
