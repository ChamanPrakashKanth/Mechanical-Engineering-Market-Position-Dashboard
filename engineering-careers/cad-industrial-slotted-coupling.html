Feature layout and design notes

Opposed axial relief slots are rotated 90 degrees relative to each other and leave a 7 mm central web. Two radial pilot passages open from the bore to the exterior. These holes are not threaded. This is not a torque-rated flexible coupling: stiffness, fatigue, balance, retention, and shaft-fit behavior are unvalidated.
