Feature layout and design notes

One L-shaped cover with a plain contact face, no magnets or snap latch. Print two copies for opposing jaws. The 12 mm overhang is an open backing space, not a validated fit to a particular vise. The original steel jaws must remain installed. It does not attach securely by itself: check retention and interference. Use only for light manual positioning trials, not hammering, grinding or powered cutting. Print on the exterior broad contact face: rotate the model 90 degrees about X so the L section opens upward; confirm bed contact in your slicer. All apertures are rectangular slots, not modeled threads. Dimensions are nominal and require printer-specific fit allowance. These original studies have not been physically printed or tested for holding force, accuracy, creep, temperature, coolant resistance or wear. Use for bench layout and fit evaluation only; no powered machining or load rating is established. Check the actual assembly and material before use.
