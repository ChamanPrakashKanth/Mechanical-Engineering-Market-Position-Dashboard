Feature layout and design notes

Smooth Y-axis Ø12 rod bore at X30/Z20 with a 2 mm slit extending upward to the top surface. A transverse Ø5.5 bolt passage runs along X at Y18/Z30; it is unthreaded and requires separately selected bolt/nut hardware. Four vertical Ø5.5 mounting holes lie at X10/50 and Y8/28. The remaining material below the bore connects both clamp halves. Rod tolerance, clamp flexure, bolt access, grip force and conveyor hygiene suitability are unvalidated; it is not a completed guide-rail system.
