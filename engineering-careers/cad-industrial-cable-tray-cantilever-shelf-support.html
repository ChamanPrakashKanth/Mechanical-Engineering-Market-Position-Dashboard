Feature layout and design notes

Original wall-mounted cantilever layout study with a slotted upright plate, horizontal tray shelf and two side gussets. The 8.5 mm wall-plate slots are centered at Y12/48 and Z16/74; the arm reaches 120 mm from the plate. The outer shelf tab includes two Ø8.5 vertical clearance holes. This is not a rated support: verify anchor type, substrate, section stiffness, buckling, load distribution, tray width, cable mass, corrosion protection and required support spacing.
