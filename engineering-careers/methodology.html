Engineering Benchmark & Scoring Methodology

This page explains how the score formula and generated comparison pool work. Benchmark profiles are deterministic simulations, not real or anonymized candidates. The assessment runs in your browser.

1. Simulated Benchmark Pool Sizes

The displayed pool sizes are deterministic model sizes used to calculate relative ranks. They do not represent collected applicant records:

  • Mechanical Engineering: Compared with a generated pool sized at 100,000 simulated profiles.
  • 11 Other Engineering Disciplines: Each of the 11 other domains uses a generated pool sized at 50,000 simulated profiles.
  • Total Represented Cohort: 100,000 + (11 × 50,000) = 650,000 modeled profiles across the 12 pools.
  • Data source: These profiles are generated from fixed domain templates and a seeded random process; they are not sourced from people, resumes, surveys, or hiring records.

2. Readiness Score Formula

Rather than an unexplainable score, the Market Readiness Index is calculated via an explicit multi-factor weighted formula:

Readiness = 20% academics + 45% skills/software + 35% practical evidence
  • Academic & Institution Factor (20%): Combines the selected institution tier (60%) and degree level (40%), then weights that academic score at 20%.
  • Technical & Software Coverage (45%): Combines the detected domain-skill score (55%) and software score (45%); this skills/software subtotal contributes 45% of readiness.
  • Practical Evidence Factor (35%): The subtotal is capped at 100 and uses project count plus a fixed baseline; resume mode adds a small signal when internship language is detected. This text match does not verify work history.

3. Percentile & Ranking Computation

Every assessment evaluates your computed Readiness Score against the sorted seeded, simulated score pool:

  • Global Domain Standing: Your rank within the generated pool size shown for the selected domain (50,000 or 100,000 simulated profiles).
  • Peer Tier Standing: Your rank among generated profiles assigned to the same university tier.
  • Specialty Cluster Standing: Your rank among specialists in your focus area (e.g. Aerodynamics vs Structural vs EV Powertrain).

4. Browser-Based Resume Privacy

The assessment is client-side:

  • PDF parsing: Resume PDFs are parsed locally via Mozilla PDF.js. The assessment code does not send resume text to a MechIntel backend.
  • Network requests: Assessment inputs are processed in the browser. Your browser still requests the site and static assets from their hosting and CDN providers.
  • Browser storage: This assessment page does not save profiles to local storage or use a MechIntel account system. The main dashboard may save progress in browser storage; review the privacy explanation before entering personal details.
