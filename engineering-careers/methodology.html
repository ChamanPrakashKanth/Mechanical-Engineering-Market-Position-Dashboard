Engineering Benchmark & Zero-Retention Architecture

Transparent methodology detailing how our 650,000 candidate benchmark database was established, how percentiles are mathematically computed, and how our client-side privacy architecture guarantees zero retention.

1. Benchmark Cohort Scale (650,000 Records)

To ensure robust percentile resolution and defensible peer comparisons:

  • Mechanical Engineering: Benchmarked against a 100,000 candidate dataset, preserving alignment with our original MechIntel candidate distribution.
  • 11 Other Engineering Disciplines: Each domain (Civil, Electrical, Electronics, Computer, Chemical, Aerospace, Automotive, Biomedical, Environmental, Industrial, Mechatronics) is benchmarked against at least 50,000 anonymized candidate records.
  • Total Represented Cohort: 100,000 + (11 × 50,000) = 650,000 total candidate records.
  • Strict PII Sanitization: All names, email addresses, phone numbers, and direct identifiers have been excised from the benchmark distributions.

2. Multi-Dimensional Scoring Mathematical Model

Rather than an unexplainable score, the Market Readiness Index is calculated via an explicit multi-factor weighted formula:

Readiness Score = (Academic & Tier Weight × 0.20) + (Core Technical & Software Coverage × 0.45) + (Practical Evidence & Artifacts × 0.35)
  • Academic & Institution Factor (20%): Evaluates degree level and university tier context (Tier 1, Tier 2, Tier 3).
  • Technical & Software Coverage (45%): Evaluates demonstrated engineering science foundations (55%) and computational/CAD tool proficiency (45%).
  • Practical Evidence Factor (35%): Calibrates tangible proof—completed projects (20 pts each), verified internships (30 pts each), industry certifications (15 pts each), and research/competition milestones.

3. Percentile & Ranking Computation

Every assessment evaluates your computed Readiness Score against the sorted empirical cohort:

  • Global Domain Standing: Your exact rank out of 50,000 candidates (or 100,000 for Mechanical).
  • Peer Tier Standing: Your percentile specifically among candidates from the same university tier.
  • Specialty Cluster Standing: Your rank among specialists in your focus area (e.g. Aerodynamics vs Structural vs EV Powertrain).

4. Zero-Retention Client-Side Architecture

Your privacy is guaranteed:

  • 100% In-Browser Execution: PDF parsing is handled locally via Mozilla PDF.js. Text never leaves your browser tab.
  • No Network Transmission: Assessment inputs (resume text, selected skills, sliders) are never sent over the network to any server.
  • No Cookies or Tracking: We do not store cookies, session identifiers, tracking telemetry, or local storage profiles.
