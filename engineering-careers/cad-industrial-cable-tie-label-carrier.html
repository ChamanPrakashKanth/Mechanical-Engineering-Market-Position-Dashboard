Feature layout and design notes

Recessed flat field for a separately supplied adhesive paper label or compatible marker, with two tie passages outside the writing area. The label field is X10–46/Y3–17 mm. Route separate soft ties through each end passage; check cable curvature, abrasion and tie tension. This is an identification carrier, not strain relief, a conductor insulator or a certified electrical marker. No cover, ink or adhesive is supplied; no durability against oils, UV or heat is established. Print broad flat underside down, recess upward. Original geometry, not a supplier replacement. All mounting apertures are rectangular slots. No physical print, optical alignment, retention, environmental, flame or electrical tests have been performed. Dimensions are nominal: measure the actual hardware and add process-specific allowance. No standards compliance or safety-rated sensing use is claimed.
