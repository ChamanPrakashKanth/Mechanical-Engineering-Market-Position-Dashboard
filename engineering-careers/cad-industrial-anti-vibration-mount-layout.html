Feature layout and design notes

Concentric layout comprises a base flange, annular elastomer placeholder and top plate, with Ø12 axial passage and four Ø8.5 base clearances on a 52 mm bolt circle. The plain cylindrical middle does not represent a compound rubber-metal bond or stiffness curve. No isolation efficiency, resonance, compression, shear, fatigue, temperature, chemical compatibility or load rating is claimed. Select a tested mount from measured excitation and equipment data.

difference(){union(){cylinder(d=base_diameter,h=base_height);translate([0,0,base_height]) annulus(elastomer_diameter,elastomer_height);translate([0,0,base_height+elastomer_height]) cylinder(d=top_plate_diameter,h=top_plate_height);}translate([0,0,-1]) cylinder(d=passage,h=base_height+elastomer_height+top_plate_height+2);for(a=[45:90:315]) translate([bolt_circle/2*cos(a),bolt_circle/2*sin(a),-1]) cylinder(d=bolt_hole,h=base_height+2);}
