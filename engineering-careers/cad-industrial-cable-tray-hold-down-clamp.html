Feature layout and design notes

Bridge-shaped clamp concept with a raised top strap and two feet containing 7 mm-wide slots with 16 mm center travel. The top is intended only to illustrate a hold-down contact surface over a tray side rail; the model does not define a tray profile, bolt, nut, spring action, clamp force or vibration retention. Check the actual tray geometry, edge distance, galvanic compatibility, fastener preload, uplift, seismic requirements and applicable installation instructions.
