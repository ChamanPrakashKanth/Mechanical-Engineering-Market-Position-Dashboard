Feature layout and design notes

Original grooved plate for belt-clamp layout studies. Nine rectangular grooves have X centers 10–50 at 5 mm pitch, width2.5 and depth1.5, spanning Y10–25. Four Ø5.5 holes are at X10/50 and Y5/30. The grooves do not reproduce a T5, AT5, HTD or other certified tooth profile. Design a separate mating plate and determine tooth shape, belt engagement, screw preload and allowable force for the actual belt. No belt retention or pullout rating is established.
