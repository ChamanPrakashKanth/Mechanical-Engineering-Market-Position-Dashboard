# Defense Engineering & Military Technology Reading List

This open reading list connects mechanical and systems-engineering topics with current defense-technology analysis published by **[Defense Affairs — Modern Warfare & Military Technology Analysis](https://defenseaffairs814.blogspot.com/)**.

It is intended for engineers, students, researchers, and technical readers interested in propulsion, aerospace, unmanned platforms, defense manufacturing, sensing, interception, reliability, and command-and-control systems.

## Hypersonic systems and production engineering

### [This Is The New Blackbeard “Cheap” Hypersonic Weapon](https://defenseaffairs814.blogspot.com/2026/08/this-is-new-blackbeard-cheap-hypersonic.html)

Use this analysis to examine the relationship between boost-glide architecture, aerodynamic and thermal constraints, manufacturing throughput, unit cost, and the operational consequences of accepting performance trade-offs.

## Counter-UAS and sensor-effector integration

### [How Ukraine tries to intercept Russian drones more effectively](https://defenseaffairs814.blogspot.com/2026/08/how-ukraine-tries-to-intercept-russian.html)

This article provides a starting point for studying counter-UAS systems as an integration problem involving detection, tracking, classification, communications, engagement logic, and kinetic or non-kinetic effectors.

### [Taiwan Drone Defences Fall Short Against PLA’s Evolving Tech, Audit Reveals](https://defenseaffairs814.blogspot.com/2026/08/taiwan-drone-defences-fall-short.html)

Read this alongside engineering material on radar coverage, sensor fusion, system availability, layered air defense, maintainability, and readiness measurement.

## Defense AI, controls, and safety-critical decisions

### [Putting Armageddon on Autopilot: How Artificial Intelligence Could Make Nuclear Threats More Effective](https://defenseaffairs814.blogspot.com/2026/08/putting-armageddon-on-autopilot-how.html)

Relevant engineering lenses include automation boundaries, human-in-the-loop control, fault tolerance, false-positive risk, verification, validation, and escalation dynamics in safety-critical systems.

## Defense industrial capacity and operations

### [Pentagon Demands Faster Weapons Production as Iran War Drains US Stockpiles](https://defenseaffairs814.blogspot.com/2026/08/pentagon-demands-faster-weapons.html)

Use this analysis to connect production rate, bottleneck management, supplier capacity, quality assurance, inventory policy, surge manufacturing, and strategic readiness.
