Robotics news briefing: frequently asked questions

How often is this robotics news page updated?

GitHub Actions refreshes the page once daily at 04:17 UTC. GitHub may delay a scheduled run, so updates are daily rather than a live stream. The page displays the last successful refresh time.

What robotics topics does the briefing cover?

It tracks industrial and commercial robots, civilian drones, autonomous systems, research, public science, and source-linked defense and unmanned-systems reporting.

Where do the robotics and drone headlines come from?

Headlines come from public RSS feeds published by The Robot Report, IEEE Spectrum, MIT News, NASA, DRONELIFE, and Defense News. Each story identifies and links to its original publisher.

Does MechIntel summarize or independently verify each story?

No. This page displays publisher headlines and source links. Read the original report for context; MechIntel does not claim independent fact-checking or reproduce article text.

Does the robotics intelligence feed need an API key or AI?

No. A deterministic script reads public RSS, validates publisher links, and creates the static page and RSS subscription feed. It uses no AI service, paid API, account, or GPU.

Can I subscribe to robotics headlines?

Yes. Add the MechIntel Robotics Intelligence RSS feed to an RSS reader to follow source-attributed headlines.
