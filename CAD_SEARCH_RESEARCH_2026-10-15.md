# Industrial CAD search research — 15 October 2026

## Evidence and limits

Researched generic CAD download queries, specific industrial component names, and editable OpenSCAD variants. No keyword-volume database or advertising account was available. Search results establish existing supply and practical use, not monthly search counts or keyword difficulty. None of these terms can honestly be labeled proven high-volume/low-competition from this research alone.

Broad manufacturer CAD queries face established supplier catalogs. The opportunity hypothesis is narrower: original editable educational geometry, explicit dimensions, immediate STL/source downloads, and honest interface limitations. Measure relevant impressions and clicks in Search Console before expanding a family.

| Cable drag chain slotted mounting bracket CAD | Maker Hardware documents slotted brackets for several chain sizes; igus provides CAD for chain mounting components | Generic CAD is competitive. Explicit slot travel and adaptable hole layout may help longer searches; no verified keyword difficulty. |

A generic clearance plate with editable cup mounting slots is a plausible narrower topic; payload and vacuum performance are unvalidated. |
| Floating bearing support block OpenSCAD | Tretter documents floating pillow-block bearing units and provides CAD | Commercial bearing assemblies dominate broad queries. An editable housing study with seat depth and bore coordinates can serve design learning; it is not a replacement part. |
