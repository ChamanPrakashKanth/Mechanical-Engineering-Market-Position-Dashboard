# Industrial CAD search research — 15 October 2026

## Evidence and limits

Researched generic CAD download queries, specific industrial component names, and editable OpenSCAD variants. No keyword-volume database or advertising account was available. Search results establish existing supply and practical use, not monthly search counts or keyword difficulty. None of these terms can honestly be labeled proven high-volume/low-competition from this research alone.

Broad manufacturer CAD queries face established supplier catalogs. The opportunity hypothesis is narrower: original editable educational geometry, explicit dimensions, immediate STL/source downloads, and honest interface limitations. Measure relevant impressions and clicks in Search Console before expanding a family.

| Selected model / target phrase | Practical demand signal | Competition and opportunity hypothesis |
|---|---|---|
| Adjustable M12 sensor bracket OpenSCAD STL | ifm sells an M12 angle bracket; Festo and Keyence publish sensor-bracket CAD resources | Supplier catalogs compete strongly on exact replacement parts. Custom dimensions and editable source address a different design task; search volume remains unknown. |
| Cable drag chain slotted mounting bracket CAD | Maker Hardware documents slotted brackets for several chain sizes; igus provides CAD for chain mounting components | Generic CAD is competitive. Explicit slot travel and adaptable hole layout may help longer searches; no verified keyword difficulty. |
| Vacuum gripper adjustable tooling plate CAD | Schmalz offers mounting plates for round tooling and vacuum CAD resources | Exact commercial tooling competes strongly. A generic clearance plate with editable cup mounting slots is a plausible narrower topic; payload and vacuum performance are unvalidated. |
| Floating bearing support block OpenSCAD | Tretter documents floating pillow-block bearing units and provides CAD | Commercial bearing assemblies dominate broad queries. An editable housing study with seat depth and bore coordinates can serve design learning; it is not a replacement part. |

## Second batch — clamps, mounts and sensor protection

Compared 12 queries across generic industrial CAD terms and narrower editable/parametric/OpenSCAD phrases. Narrow phrases often returned sparse or irrelevant results. This is a potential content gap, not proof of low keyword difficulty: sparse results can also indicate low demand. No monthly search counts were available. Broad timing-belt clamp searches were visibly more competitive, with supplier catalogs and existing parametric models.

| Selected original study | Evidence of use | Search positioning hypothesis |
|---|---|---|
| Timing belt clamp plate with editable grooves | MISUMI, Robotunits and SDP/SI offer belt clamps and CAD | Serve custom plate layout and groove editing; do not promise standard tooth compatibility. Broad belt-clamp CAD is competitive. |
| Pneumatic cylinder slotted foot mount | Festo publishes foot-mount dimensions and CAD | Offer explicit opening, four-hole pattern and mounting travel for a custom bracket study. Generic cylinder CAD is competitive; editable bracket phrasing was sparse. |
| Sensor protective hood with slotted base | ifm lists protective sensor covers and brackets | Answer clearance, hood depth and mounting-slot questions with original geometry. Narrow OpenSCAD search had no clearly relevant hit; demand remains unknown. |
| Conveyor guide rod split clamp block | CRS VAMIC provides guide-rail clamp CAD in several formats | Address smooth-rod passage, slit and bolt layout. Existing supplier and printable clamp models compete; custom source and exact layout are the intended value. |
