Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the MechIntel benchmarking algorithm.

How is the Employability Score calculated?

Our algorithm scores profiles out of 100 using a balanced weight matrix: Academics (25% based on college tier and degree), Skills & Tools (35% based on breadth and tool usage), Experience (30% based on internships, project counts, and FSAE/Baja engineering competitions), and Extras (10% based on publications and certificates).

Is my resume data safe?

Yes. Your privacy is our priority. All parsing is done locally in your browser using pdf.js, and personally identifiable information (PII) like names, email addresses, phone numbers, and social URLs are automatically stripped out before running analysis. No resume data is saved on external databases.

How is the 100,000 competitor database sourced?

The dataset represents entry-level mechanical engineering candidates, seeded and scaled deterministically in the client environment using a Linear Congruential Generator. It models real-world distributions of freshers in regions like India and major global hubs across college tiers and specialties.
