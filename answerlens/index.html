    Open about the limits

    How is the SEO and AEO score calculated?

    AnswerLens is a deterministic checker, not a generative AI model. Each scored check has a visible weight of 1–3 points. Passing earns those points; a review earns zero. Not-checked items are excluded. Paragraph-length and word-count thresholds are product heuristics, not rules published by search engines. Question detection currently uses English patterns.

    Score = earned points ÷ available tested points × 100

    URL mode inspects fetched HTML and response headers. Paste/upload mode inspects supplied HTML. Text mode checks only substantive text coverage; it does not infer missing markup. These modes do not render JavaScript, verify accuracy, crawl linked pages, measure backlinks, confirm indexing or test actual AI citations.

    Does AEO require special AI markup?

    No special AI markup is required for Google's AI search features. Google's guidance says SEO best practices remain relevant. Structured data should be appropriate and agree with visible content; FAQ markup or an llms.txt file does not guarantee visibility.

    References: Google: AI features and your website · SEO Starter Guide · Structured data guidelines

    Before you audit

    Common questions

    Is the checker really free?

    Yes. This version has no account, paid plan or paid AI API. Public scans have a best-effort five-per-minute limit per address to reduce abuse. Hosting limits may affect availability. Paste/upload analysis runs locally.

    Will a high score get my website cited by AI?

    No. A score describes only our tested signals. Relevance, accuracy, reputation, indexing and the engine's query-dependent choices also matter. Real visibility must be measured separately.

    Why did my URL scan fail or find little text?

    Some sites block automated requests or generate content with JavaScript. This checker does not bypass those restrictions or run a browser. Try an HTML export in paste/upload mode. Public HTTP(S) domains only, no custom ports or query strings, up to three redirects and 1 MB HTML.

    Can I check a page before publishing?

    Yes. Paste or upload its HTML. Add the intended page URL to resolve relative links. Results cannot confirm live HTTP status, headers or indexing.

    Privacy & data handling

    Paste/upload content and reports stay in this browser tab unless downloaded. We do not save them in browser storage. URL mode sends the public URL to the free AnswerLens backend at https://answerlens-seo-aeo.quirky-bream-4366.chatgpt.site and requests that page from its host; public DNS lookup uses Cloudflare. Do not submit private URLs or confidential content.

    The application has no database, analytics scripts, cookies or advertising scripts. URL scans use short-lived in-memory rate counters and do not intentionally persist HTML or reports. Hosting providers and destination websites may process operational request logs. External validation links send the page URL to the named service when opened.
