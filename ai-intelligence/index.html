Evidence coverage and assessment rules

Stage assignments are editorial assessments within each exact definition. Models, software and IP follow the originating organization; contributors may be global. Fabrication, packaging and physical infrastructure require in-country operations. Access to imported GPUs does not establish domestic manufacturing. No overall country score is calculated.
