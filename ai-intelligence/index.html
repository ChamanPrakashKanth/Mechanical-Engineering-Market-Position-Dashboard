What this view measures

Models, software and IP are attributed to the originating organization, with global contributors acknowledged. Fabrication and infrastructure require physical in-country operations. Cloud access may use imported hardware.

Adoption of shared or foreign-origin tools is not assessed here. A gap in domestic-origin supply says nothing about whether a country can use PyTorch, retrieval, MLOps or other global tools. Source counts measure research coverage; they are not country scores.
